Round 13 · 13a · the looks become a setting

Four themes, one layout

Sticking with 11: rather than choosing one look, all of them ship. Stone is 10a's dark glass, and 11a Vellum, 11b Steel and 11c Hairline join it as themes picked in Options. A theme may only change five things — surface, rule, ink, accent, face — so every layout rule, size and hit target stays identical and a theme can never break a screen. That is also what makes it cheap: one token set read by both builds.
