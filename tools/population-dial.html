The game's own bench, asked a different question. Nothing here models anything.

Not wired yet. Children per household, shortest life and longest life are read from module constants at import — `LIFE.CHILDREN`, `LIFE.SHORTEST_LIFE`, `LIFE.LONGEST_LIFE` — by `births.ts` and `people.ts`, so a slider here would move the number and change nothing. Deliberately absent rather than present and lying.

`roofLadder(children)` in `world/roofs.ts` is the shape each of them has to take: derived when asked rather than frozen when the module loads. It is done and tested; threading it through `holdsFor` and `foundVillage` is the next step, and then this panel grows three sliders.

Move a dial and press the button. Three seeds, eight villages each; a hundred days takes a few seconds and four hundred takes rather more.
